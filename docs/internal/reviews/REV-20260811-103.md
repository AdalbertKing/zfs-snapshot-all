# REV-20260811-103 — source-retention design grant/bookmark premises

## Scope

Independent design-time review of the REV-102 source-retention plan. The original review found two P1 premises that had to be corrected before implementing destructive remote source pruning.

## F1 — stale grant diagnosis

The original design claimed the PULL source lacked `destroy` and proposed widening the grant with `destroy,mount`. Current `deploy.sh` already delegates `destroy` at the relationship scope. The correction at `49d547aeb444259225a9aa4f9bae721ca6bf21f4` removes the stale widening instructions and defines step 3 as remote-prune composition plus fail-closed verification of the existing delegated `destroy` capability and pinned SSH path. Negative control remains: revoke/omit `destroy` and prove refusal/failure.

**Verified: corrected.** No new privilege is justified by this path unless live command-path evidence proves one is required.

## F2 — bookmark/default-ladder guarantee overstated

The original design treated the per-target bookmark/default retention window as a guarantee of incremental continuity. Current runtime does not support that claim: bookmark fallback is conditional, bookmark refresh is best-effort, and ordinary common snapshots can also age out after failure sequences.

The correction at `49d547aeb444259225a9aa4f9bae721ca6bf21f4` now states the correct invariant: incremental continuity is guaranteed only while a usable ordinary common snapshot or proven usable bookmark remains. Equal default retention reduces risk but does not prove survival through every sequence; REV-102's live/negative-control campaign explicitly includes bookmark-unavailable + later snapshot creation/prune.

**Verified: corrected.** The remaining recursive-policy wording and the structural independence of source/target prune templates are separate current-main findings tracked in REV-20260811-104.

## Related REV-102 partial

The same implementation also corrects the local source-prune scope mismatch: current non-recursive `[dataset:<root>]` now gets a non-recursive source prune (no `-R`), while target pruning remains recursive. The targeted regression distinguishes the source line from the recursive target line. This is accepted as the narrow local correction; REV-102 itself remains OPEN for remote composition, migration/audit, and live-ZFS evidence.

## Verdict

APPROVED at `49d547aeb444259225a9aa4f9bae721ca6bf21f4` for the two premises REV-103 defined. This approval does not close REV-102 and does not approve the distinct findings in REV-104.
