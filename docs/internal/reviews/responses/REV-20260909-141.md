# Response to REV-20260909-141

## F1 — IMPLEMENTED

### Analysis

Accepted in full. The `PLAN_PRUNE_LEGACY` branch removed the peer-root
`[prune:TARGET/<peer>]` and wrote the per-landing replacements through
`profile_emit "$PROFILE_PRUNE_FILE"` — the profile on disk today. That is the
one place in the migration where installed CONFIG truth was re-derived, and the
reviewer's fixture shows the cost exactly: `gfs_pattern = automated_hourly`
came back as the profile's `automated_`, widening the family the GFS delete
engine may prune, with rc=0 and the fifth clobber-guard exemption hiding the
swap. Every other re-activation path (the REV-089 `[dataset:]` preservation,
the REV-107 source-prune body replay) already carries the installed body; the
migration was the gap.

### Implemented change

`capture_legacy_ladder_policy <file> <header>` reads the legacy section's body
BEFORE `remove_managed_sections` runs, keeping every directive except the
managed-by marker and the three topology-owned lines (`recursive`,
`pair_label`, `notify`). In the per-landing loop, when a body was captured it
is printed verbatim under `[prune:<landing>]` in place of `profile_emit` and the
stagger schedule; only `recursive` (a flat landing is not a subtree),
`pair_label` and `notify` (named after the landing) are recomputed, exactly as
for a fresh section. A legacy header the planner names whose body cannot be
read back **refuses** the migration (`die`), never falls back to the profile.

Acceptance criteria, one by one:

1. replacement policy from the installed section — yes, byte-for-byte body;
2. `use_template`, `gfs`, `gfs_pattern`, `prune_schedule`, `keep`/`retain`,
   `monitor_*`, `quiesce`, `monitor_exclude` all survive; recomputed only
   `recursive`, `pair_label`, `notify`;
3. discriminator with a valid legacy ladder whose `gfs_pattern` differs from
   the profile — `gfsshape` 6b, judged on the REAL `gen-cron.sh` output;
4. the existing `gfsshape` controls are untouched and green;
5. the clobber-guard exemption is unchanged (`line_scope_narrowed_by_migration`
   and its three controls untouched).
