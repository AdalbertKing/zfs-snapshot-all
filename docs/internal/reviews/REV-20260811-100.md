# REV-20260811-100 — REV-099 regression does not exercise the required pointer-advance lifecycle

## Scope

Review-protocol test coverage only. The durable `reviewed-by:` records added to `DELIVERIES.md` are directionally correct and the current generated view no longer resurfaces the already-reviewed Phase 3.5/4/5/6 deliveries. No production-code defect is alleged.

## Finding F1 — the new d8/d9 proof skips the state transition it claims to pin

REV-099 required one deterministic regression proving the actual lifecycle:

1. a direct-main delivery starts OPEN;
2. a review of that delivery exists at the delivery SHA;
3. a follow-up advances the review's `reviewed-implementation` to a later cumulative SHA;
4. after closure + generate, the original delivery stays absent from OPEN-THREADS;
5. an actually unreviewed delivery remains OPEN.

The implementation `c5b7c3f05a06c88724e5151181eb62d4905e8953` does not exercise that sequence.

In d8/d9 the test does:

- `deliver "$sha1"`;
- immediately creates an APPROVED review at `$sha2`;
- responds/closes at `$sha2`;
- manually appends `reviewed-by: $sha1`.

There is no assertion that the direct-main delivery starts OPEN, no review state that ever names `$sha1`, and no transition that moves the same review from `$sha1` to `$sha2`. The comments and Claude response describe a pointer advance, but the executable fixture jumps directly to the final cumulative SHA.

The older d6/d7 cases also do not supply the missing transition: they create a delivery at `$sha1` while the review already names `$sha2`; they prove that `reviewed-by` can suppress a delivery whose current review pointer is elsewhere, but not that a reviewed delivery survives the actual advance lifecycle required by REV-099.

This matters because the historical failure is specifically temporal/stateful. A test that starts only in the final state can remain green if a future change breaks the transition or its bookkeeping while still accepting a manually pre-populated final `reviewed-by` marker.

## Required correction — minimal

Replace or extend d8 with one compact lifecycle fixture that actually performs and asserts the states in order:

- create `delivered: $sha1` and generate; assert `$sha1` is OPEN/DELIVERED;
- open the REV against `$sha1` and generate; assert the delivery row is replaced by the REV row;
- advance the same review to `$sha2` through the repository's normal review/follow-up representation;
- record the durable `reviewed-by: $sha1 REV-...` fact at the appropriate point;
- close at `$sha2`, generate, and assert both the original delivery and closed REV are absent;
- keep the existing genuinely-unreviewed `$sha3` negative control.

Use the existing fixture helpers; do not redesign `reviewctl`. The target is one state-transition regression, not more PASS count.

No ZFS, SSH, crontab, deploy, or broad suite is required. `test/reviewctl` plus the dependency-selected protocol cascade is sufficient.

## Verdict

CHANGES REQUIRED (P2 process/test-proof). REV-099 cannot be approved yet because its required regression is not actually exercised. This does **not** block Phase 5 slice 2 functional implementation; it blocks closure of the review-bookkeeping fix until the lifecycle proof is load-bearing.
