# REV-20260811-099 — reviewed deliveries resurface in OPEN-THREADS after cumulative approval

## Scope

Review-protocol / bookkeeping only. No production-code defect is alleged.

## Finding F1 — CLOSED reviews do not retire the original direct-main deliveries

Fresh `main` at review: `90ff78f57e8380ae03914632f16d192c531ae668`.

`reviewctl --generate` now correctly shows REV-097 and REV-098 as CLOSED, but the generated `docs/project/OPEN-THREADS.md` still tells the reviewer to review these already-reviewed deliveries:

- `9074fe55` Phase 4 slice 1 — reviewed through REV-095, now CLOSED;
- `5df74522` Phase 6 examples — reviewed through REV-094/096, now CLOSED;
- `f8ec3eae` Phase 5 slice 1 — reviewed through REV-097/098, now CLOSED.

`docs/project/DELIVERIES.md` still has bare `delivered:` records for those SHAs and no corresponding `reviewed-by:` records. This becomes visible when a review's `reviewed-implementation` advances to a cumulative follow-up SHA: the original delivery SHA is no longer matched and reappears as an unreviewed delivery.

That recreates the duplicate-review / stale-routing failure mode REVIEW PROTOCOL V2 was introduced to eliminate. A generated view that says both "REV CLOSED" and "review this delivery" is not a harmless cosmetic inconsistency.

## Required correction — minimal

1. Retire the three already-reviewed delivery records above with explicit, durable linkage to their reviews (or an equivalent canonical mechanism).
2. Make the linkage survive a cumulative implementation-pointer advance. The original delivery must not become OPEN again merely because a follow-up moves the reviewed implementation SHA.
3. Add one small deterministic regression around `reviewctl`/delivery bookkeeping that proves:
   - a direct-main delivery starts OPEN;
   - a review of it can advance to a later cumulative implementation through a follow-up;
   - after closure + generate, the original delivery is absent from OPEN-THREADS;
   - an actually unreviewed delivery remains OPEN as the negative control.

Do not broaden this into production tests, ZFS, SSH, crontab, deploy, or a full campaign. The defect boundary is pure review bookkeeping.

## Non-finding note

The current `PROJECT_STATUS` does contain the Phase 5 `localbackup` suite/status material and the active work plan correctly keeps slice 2 as the next functional step. Its older high-level "one-host orchestration = not implemented" wording is interpretable as the still-incomplete end-to-end workflow because slice 1 is read-only; no separate finding is opened for that wording here.

## Reviewer follow-up — APPROVED

Implementation `c5b7c3f05a06c88724e5151181eb62d4905e8953` supplies the durable bookkeeping fix: the historical direct-main delivery SHAs are explicitly retired with `reviewed-by:` records, so later movement of a REV's `reviewed-implementation` pointer cannot make them reappear.

The first regression submitted with REV-099 asserted too much of the lifecycle only in its final shape; that independent proof defect was tracked as REV-100 and corrected in `53e2cc61d096c9a165fb3cb1de4eb021267abda4`, now CLOSED. The corrected test walks the required temporal sequence: delivery at `sha1` is OPEN, the REV replaces it at `sha1`, advancing the same REV to cumulative `sha2` makes the old delivery resurface, the durable marker retires it, closure leaves both it and the REV absent, while a genuinely unreviewed `sha3` remains OPEN.

Current `DELIVERIES.md` contains the durable markers for the reviewed Phase 3.5, Phase 4, Phase 5 slice 1 and Phase 6 deliveries. The current generated routing contains no resurfaced delivery rows. No production/runtime boundary changed, so no ZFS/SSH/crontab/account/grant proof is relevant.

## Verdict

**APPROVED.** REV-099's bookkeeping property is satisfied; REV-100 supplies the strengthened discriminating regression evidence without changing the underlying fix.
